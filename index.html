I am a PhD student at EPFL in the Signal Processing Laboratory 4 (LTS4), advised by Prof. Pascal Frossard.

My research interests are in AI safety and alignment. My work explores backdoor unlearning, model editing, and the safety and post-training of diffusion language models.
